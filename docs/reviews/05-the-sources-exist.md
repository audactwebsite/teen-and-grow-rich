# Review 5 — "The one place this site breaks its own rule"

Received 2 August 2026. The only review that went looking for outside evidence rather than
reading the site and reasoning about it.

**It closes the single open hole in the project**, and that is worth more than everything
else in all seven reviews combined, because it is the one place where the site currently
fails a rule it applies to everyone else.

---

## The finding: `/proof` can now be sourced

Non-negotiable 10 says: *every number links to a primary source, or it gets cut.* Site-wide.

`/proof` has been the exception. It states Ryan's karting record — first kart at eight, a
debut at Spa, a Belgian title at ten, a win over a world champion — and links to nothing,
because nobody had gone looking. It was on Ryan's list as item 7, blocked on the family
supplying evidence.

The review says the record is already documented in Dutch motorsport press. **Checked, and
it is.** Every claim on `/proof` resolves to independent published journalism that predates
this project by years and was written by people with no stake in a book:

Also found: a [BMC podium](https://parkstadactueel.nl/2022/06/23/karttalent-ryan-rijvers-scoort-alweer-podiumplaats-in-de-bmc/) and a
[kart marathon podium after a recovery drive](https://www.racexpress.nl/chrono/ryan-rijvers-doet-opnieuw-een-kartmarathon-en-snelt-naar-podium-na-inhaalrace-heel-trots/n/124782), neither of which the site claims.

**Adopt, and it is now the top of the build list.** `/proof` gets a source line per claim,
in the same form the rest of the site uses. This is not decoration — it is the page that
tells a reader why to believe a thirteen-year-old, and until today it was the one page
asking for trust it had not earned by its own standard.

### Two corrections the sources force

1. **The German win was in Minimax, not Mini Rookie.** The Belgian title was Mini Rookie.
   These are different classes and the site's summary blurs them. Fixed on `/proof` and in
   `CLAUDE.md`.
2. **The Spa result is "fourth overall", not a podium.** The site already says fourth. Kept
   — the three trophies are the interesting part, and the smaller true version is the one
   this project takes every time.

### What is still not sourced

The **F4 car built to fit him at eleven** appears in no press source found. `CLAUDE.md`
already forbids publishing an F4 *competition* claim, and `/proof` already says he trains
in it rather than races it — which is the honest version. But "trains in a custom F4 at
eleven" is still an unsourced statement on a page that will now carry sources beside it,
and that contrast makes it more conspicuous, not less.

**Either the family supplies something checkable, or that line goes.** Added to Ryan's list
as its own item.

## Measured, and the review is wrong

### "Ten places saying pending, coming soon or in progress"

The review calls these *the cancer of a project that has everything finished except the last
one percent*. Counted every reader-visible instance:

| Where | What it says | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `/get` | Retailer link pending | **Real blocker.** On Ryan's list |
| `/tools/scam-check`, `/tools/shrink` | Helpline finder — not in place yet | **Real blocker.** The most urgent item on Ryan's list |
| `/privacy` | Host details pending | Deliberate. Names what is not yet confirmed rather than guessing |
| `/schools/privacy` | Still open — two items, named | Deliberate. Written for an IT reviewer who will trust a named gap over a clean page |
| `/accessibility` | Not verified — six items, named | Deliberate. An accessibility statement that claims full conformance without testing is the standard lie in the field |

Six, not ten — and **four of them are the method, not a shortfall.** A site whose thesis is
*every number links to a primary source or it gets cut* has to disclose what it has not
checked. Removing those lines would make the site less honest and better-looking, which is
the trade this project exists to refuse.

The two real ones are real, and they are already the top of the owner's list.

## What this review proves about the method

The other six reviews reasoned about the site. This one went and checked something outside
it, and it was the only one that could have closed a real gap — because the gap was never
a design problem. No amount of rewriting `/proof` would have sourced it.

Worth remembering the next time a review is judged on how impressive it reads.
