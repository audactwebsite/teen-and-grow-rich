## Safety content — treat as first-class

**19 Reality Checks, and they are not one per build.** Verified by scanning every page
for the marker:

- **15** belong to the 15 chapters
- **1** belongs to the 90-Day build (printed page 56)
- **3 stand alone**, attached to no build at all:
  - printed page 2 — the Publishing Note ("Before commercial release, have the final
    title, trademark wording, legal notices, links, privacy terms, contests, and payment
    flows checked by qualified publishing and legal professionals…")
  - printed page 9 — Your Starting Point ("My rule: I will not lie about my age, bypass
    platform rules, share private details for attention, or handle risky money,
    contracts, meetings, or travel alone.")
  - printed page 57 — Seven Safe First Offers ("A safe first offer has a small scope, a
    clear adult-approved contact route, no access to private accounts, and no promise of
    results you cannot control.")

Three of the 19 builds carry no Reality Check: the Receipts folder (page 4), the 30-Day
Tiny Launch (page 59) and the Afterword (page 62).

The standalone three matter especially: the page-9 one is the reader's personal safety
pledge and belongs on the Starting Point worksheet, and the page-2 one is the book's own
instruction to get professional legal review — which the site's legal pages must honour
rather than quietly drop.

Themes across all 19: age and platform rules · paid courses
and purchases · copyright, privacy and AI uploads · contracts, customer data,
tax and shipping · urgency as a manipulation tactic · burnout and sleep · online
collaboration boundaries · courage vs. actual danger · financial advice and
under-18 accounts · privacy traded for attention · anxiety mistaken for
intuition · harassment, threats and blackmail · self-worth and family finances ·
minors' contract and payment restrictions · safe offer scope.

**For Parents & Guardians** (p61) includes the family approval system, which the
site should present as its own component:

- **Green** — low-cost and reversible. Go.
- **Yellow** — public posting, small spending, contact with new people. Check in first.
- **Red** — contracts, financial products, travel, identity documents, large payments, in-person meetings. Direct adult involvement.

Plus the family rule: *"A legitimate opportunity can survive an adult review and
a night of sleep."*
